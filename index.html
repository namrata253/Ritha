Ritha

Plant Information

Botanical Name: Sapindus mukorossi (Gaertn)

Common Name: Ritha Soapnut

Family: Sapindaceae

Description

A medium to large tree with green leaves and round fruits containing natural saponins.

Medicinal Uses

  1. Used for cleaning hair and skin ; traditionally used in some herbal preparation.
  2. Used in traditionally medicine for rheumatic pain and joint discomfort.
  3. Fruit extracts have been used traditionally for digestive complaints.
